# Anime Clash — how the top games sell (shop research and plan)

Researched 2026-10-21. Prices are from fan guides and change often; treat them as ballpark.

## What the top games sell

| Game | Permanent passes | Repeat buys (impulse) | How progression items are sold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Steal a Brainrot** | 2x Money 199, VIP 199, HD Admin 1,999 | **Lucky Blocks** by tier: Mythic 175, God 599, Secret 2,399 R$, each with a cooldown before the next | Free gear in a coin shop; better gear unlocks with rebirths; premium gear (traps, weapons) in a Robux shop on the map and behind the Shop button |
| **Steal an Egg** | 2x Growth 467, 2x Money 399 | Grow All Eggs ~160, Instant Hatch ~9 per egg | The core loop items (eggs) are earned; Robux only skips timers |
| **Kick a Lucky Block** | 2x Brainrot Luck 249, 2x Mutation Luck 349, VIP 729 (2x luck + 1.5x cash + tag) | Server Luck x2 + 15M cash 199 | Luck is the product: every pass is a luck multiplier |
| **Grow a Garden** | — | **Restock now** ~39 R$ | The seed and gear shops restock on a ~5-minute timer with random, rarity-weighted stock; rare items are scarce |
| **Fisch / RIVALS / Unboxing Sim** | — | Starter Bundle (new players only, 49 R$ in Unboxing Sim), bundles at ~16% off | Bundles of several items cheaper than buying each |

Roblox itself now adds a platform Shop with "Top Picks" for each player and Robux packs bundled with a game's items (live July 7, 2026). Roblox's own guidance: timers and appointment mechanics that annoy players work worse on Roblox than on other platforms, so skip-the-wait products should feel like a bonus, never a wall.

## The patterns that matter
1. **Luck is the biggest seller in drop games.** Kick a Lucky Block's passes are almost all luck; Steal a Brainrot sells luck as lucky blocks. We sell Server Luck but no personal luck pass.
2. **Tiered loot items with a cooldown** (Brainrot lucky blocks): three price points (cheap, mid, whale), each a guaranteed rarity floor. This is the main impulse buy.
3. **A rotating, scarce stock** (Grow a Garden): a timer and a "restock now" button make people check back and spend.
4. **Rebirth-gated progression items:** better gear unlocks at rebirth levels, so the progression shop always has a visible next item.
5. **Cheap first purchase:** a new-player starter bundle (~49 R$) converts first-time buyers.
6. **2x Money and VIP are standard at 199–400.** VIP bundles several perks plus a visible tag.
7. **Instant skips priced per use** (Instant Hatch ~9, Grow All ~160) for impatient moments.

## Our shop today, against that
- **Have:** VIP, 2x Cash, 2x Power, Auto Train, Fast Steal, Server Luck, 2x Training (15 min), Base Shield, Cash Pack, Cash Vault, Next Stand, and the two premium weights.
- **Missing:**
  - a personal luck pass (the top seller in drop games)
  - tiered fighter "summons" bought with Robux (the lucky-block equivalent)
  - a starter pack
  - a rotating stock with a restock timer
  - rebirth-gated items
  - bundles at a discount

## Proposed shop (for approval)
**Layout:** one SHOP window with tabs along the top: FEATURED · PASSES · BOOSTS · CASH. A balance bar shows Cash and Ki.

1. **FEATURED tab (opens first).** This is the impulse corner.
   - **Starter Pack** (new players, first 3 days, big "-70%" badge): 49 R$ for an Epic fighter, Wooden→Iron weights, 2x Training for 30 min and $25K.
   - **Limited weights:** the Shenron Dragon and Nine-Tails Chakra cards (as now), with a "LEAVING IN 2D 14H" countdown that rotates them for other premium weights every few days.
   - **Fighter summons** (our lucky blocks): MYTHIC SUMMON 175, GOD SUMMON 599, SECRET SUMMON 2,399. Each is a beam that drops a fighter of at least that rarity straight onto your base, with the drop cinematic.
     - **Decision needed:** you said earlier that a summon/roll was too pay-to-win. These are the single biggest earner in Steal a Brainrot. A middle ground is to sell them only with a guaranteed fighter and no Power, since fighters only earn cash and train faster.
2. **PASSES tab:**
   - VIP (+ tag)
   - 2x Cash
   - 2x Power
   - Auto Train
   - Fast Steal
   - **x2 Drop Luck** (new, ~249: your drops roll rarer)
   - **x2 Mutation Luck** (new, ~349)
   - **+2 Stands** (new)
3. **BOOSTS tab:**
   - Server Luck
   - 2x Training 15 min
   - Base Shield
   - **Instant Restock** of the gear stall (new, ~29, if we add rotating stock)
4. **CASH tab:** Cash Pack, Cash Vault, and a bigger "Treasury" (8 hours).

**Selling progression (the gear stall):**
- **Rotating stock:** each stall shows 3 random items from the next weights, with rare ones appearing less often. A 5-minute restock timer sits at the top, with "RESTOCK NOW 29 R$" beside it.
- **Rebirth-gated items:** a few rare weights unlock only at Rebirth 1, 3 and 5, and show a lock with "REBIRTH 3".
- **Premium cards** stay pinned on the right as now.

**Buying moments (the impulse triggers):**
- **Nudges:**
  - a pop-up offer of Server Luck when a Mythic or Secret lands
  - a pop-up offer of a Base Shield right after someone steals from you
  - a pop-up offer of 2x Training after a rebirth
- **Limits:** each nudge shows at most once per session, and never during a clash.

## Build order (once approved)
1. Tabbed shop with FEATURED, plus Starter Pack and the luck passes.
2. Summons (if approved).
3. Rotating gear stock and restock timer; rebirth-gated weights.
4. Moment-based offers.

## Decided 2026-10-21
- **Summons:** yes, fighters only. MYTHIC 175, GOD 599 and SECRET 2,399 R$ each drop a fighter of at least that rarity onto your base. No Power is sold directly.
- **Gear stalls:** rotating stock of 3 weights, a 5-minute restock timer and "Restock now" for Robux.
- **New items:** all four added: Starter Pack 49 R$, x2 Drop Luck pass, moment offers (at most once each per session, never mid-clash), and rebirth-gated weights.
