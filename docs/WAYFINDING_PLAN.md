# Anime Clash — making the hub readable (wayfinding plan)

Drafted 2026-10-20 after "everything looks too cluttered to find things."

## What's wrong (from the spawn view and the aerial)
- **Everything is the same red.** Torii, roofs, the colosseum, banners, market stalls and zone gates are all vermilion, so nothing stands out as "the place to go."
- **Things of one kind are scattered.** The 6 drop altars ring the whole island; the gym, gear stall and practice dojo sit in leftover gaps; the zone gates are spread round the rim. A new player can't build a mental map.
- **Many lookalikes.** The 4 zone gates are identical tall torii. The 8 base islands are identical green squares. The market houses are identical.
- **Too much decoration at eye level.** Lanterns, banners, flags, light pillars, trees and stalls all compete with the things you use.
- **No signposts.** Nothing says "ALTARS →" or "GYM →".

## The fix: 5 districts, one colour each, one landmark each
Think of the hub as a clock face around the colosseum. Every kind of activity gets its own wedge, its own colour (used on its roofs, flags, floor and signs) and one big landmark you can see from spawn.

| District | What's in it | Colour | Landmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spawn plaza** (south) | spawn, the welcome torii, a directory signpost | white and gold | the big ANIME CLASH torii |
| **Drop Arena** (north, round the colosseum) | all 6 hub altars together in one ring, the portal above | gold | the drop portal and its light beam |
| **Training Grounds** (east) | punching posts, sandbag, gear stall, practice dojo | orange | a giant stone weight on a stone base |
| **Zone Gates** (west) | the 4 gates side by side in a row, each its zone's colour and emblem | each zone's own | the 4 coloured gates |
| **Bases** (the outer ring of islets) | the 8 plots | each plot's own colour on its torii and floor | your plot's name sign and a beam over your own base |

Spawn faces the Drop Arena, so the first thing a new player sees is the altars and the portal.

## Concrete changes
1. **Move things into their districts.**
   - The hub keeps 4 altars (2 Bronze, 2 Silver) in one ring round the colosseum; Gold and Diamond only exist in the deeper zones (decided 2026-10-20; servers hold 8 players).
   - Training gear moves into the Training Grounds.
   - The 4 zone gates line up in the Gate district.
2. **Colour-code.** Each district's floor tiles, flags and roof trim take its colour; the hub's general red stays only on the colosseum and the pagoda.
3. **Thin the decoration by about half.**
   - Keep trees, but only at district edges, so they frame spaces instead of filling them.
   - Remove most free-standing lanterns, banners and flags; keep them only along paths.
   - Remove the light pillars except the drop portal's.
4. **Clear wide paths.** A paved ring road plus one spoke road from spawn to each district, with nothing standing on them.
5. **Signposts.** A tall signpost at spawn and at each junction with icon arrows (our UI icons on wooden boards): DROPS, TRAINING, ZONES, YOUR BASE.
6. **Distinct gates and bases.**
   - Each zone gate wears its zone's colour, emblem and a scene behind it (blossom tree, ninja headband, pirate flag, wall).
   - Each base torii and floor edge uses its plot colour, with the owner's name big on top.
7. **Personal beacon (no world clutter).** Your own base gets a soft beam only you can see, and the BASE button stays. No compass bar: signposts only (decided 2026-10-20).

## Also in the zones
The same rules apply in each zone: one drop square, one training corner, one guardian path, signposts at the arrival. This is a lighter pass, since zones are already themed by their own colours.

## Build order
1. Hub layout: move altars, gym and gates into districts; clear the roads.
2. Colour-coding and decoration thinning.
3. Signposts and distinct gates and bases.
4. Personal base beam.
5. The same pass in the 4 zones.
